conference record, not its journal. No satellite or unmatched venue inherits a rank.

Four additions have defensible historical main-track acceptance evidence:
- [ACL 2025 proceedings preface](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.0.pdf): 20.3%, 1,699 main papers from 8,360 unique ARR submissions; Findings excluded.
- [EMNLP 2025 preface](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.0.pdf): 22.16%, 1,811/8,174 including desk rejections and withdrawals; Findings excluded.
- [NAACL 2025 preface](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.0.pdf): chairs' approximate 22% of intended ARR submissions. Do not substitute the smaller commitment-only denominator.
- [RecSys official statistics](https://recsys.acm.org/statistics/): 2025 long papers, 49/261, organizer-rounded 19%.

The other twelve additions remain Unknown. Historical percentages are track-
and denominator-specific, not a prediction or an interchangeable venue quality
score. Neither ranks nor accepted-only counts justify a fabricated rate.
