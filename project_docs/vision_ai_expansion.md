Reviewed: 2026-10-07. This is an evidence record for the second user-approved
AI Deadlines batch, not a new candidate recommendation or an exhaustive review

## Approved Scope And Relevance

Added 15 series: EuroGP, KR, ICAPS, and all 12 original Vision, Imaging and
Multimedia candidates. FG and IJCB are the two dedicated biometrics venues
in the remaining 46 and are included, not counted again. Total catalog:
122 editions / 97 series. The original 57-series gap now has 26 added and
31 untracked series; ICWM remains separate.

- CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV are the main broad vision flagships, as identified by
  the [Computer Vision Foundation](https://www.thecvf.com/?page_id=100).
- BMVC and WACV are established broad/applied vision venues; 3DV is focused
  on three-dimensional vision and geometry. They do not all have the same
  standing or contribution requirements as the flagship trio.
- ACM MM is the major broad multimedia venue, SIGGRAPH the graphics/interactive
  techniques flagship, and ICMR a focused multimedia-retrieval venue. These are
  distinct communities, not interchangeable computer-vision conferences.
- [FG](https://fg2027.ieee-biometrics.org/call-for-papers/) specializes in face,
  gesture, and human-body analysis; [IJCB](https://ijcb2027.ieee-biometrics.org/call-for-papers/)
  covers biometrics and identity science. IJCB combines BTAS and ICB; there
  is no separately approved BTAS/ICB candidate or duplicate row.
- EUVIP is an established specialist visual-information-processing venue,
  not a flagship inferred from its acronym or IEEE association.
- [EuroGP](https://www.evostar.org/2027/eurogp/) is an established genetic-programming
  community with evolutionary ML and symbolic-modeling relevance. [KR](https://kr.org/)
  and [ICAPS](https://icaps27.icaps-conference.org/calls/cfp/) are core AI venues
  for knowledge/reasoning and planning, respectively, not general pure-ML
  venues. ICAPS explicitly requires a planning/scheduling contribution and
  excludes reinforcement learning alone.
